NX PANEL — OPERATING MANUAL

Core Rule: Trains stop at red signals. They only move when you set a route and the signal clears.

How to play:

  1. Spawn trains (they will stop at the first red signal).
  2. Set a route using the Route Buttons or by clicking Red Entrance → Yellow Exit.
  3. Signal clears → train proceeds along the locked route.
  4. Route releases automatically behind the train.

Levels: Changing level loads a different track layout (cached locally after first use).

Casting to your 43" TV:

  1. Connect the TV as a second / extended display (HDMI), then press K or click 📺 CAST.
  2. The game opens a dedicated big-screen window on the TV and takes over the simulation.
  3. Click ⛶ FULLSCREEN once inside that window (browsers require one click for fullscreen).
  4. Your keyboard and mouse now drive the TV view — click signals, press S / Space / 1-2-4 exactly as before.
  5. Press ✕ in the TV window to hand control back to this console.
  6. No second display? Press F for in-window big-screen mode.

Keyboard: Space = Pause, 1/2/4 = Speed, S = Spawn, C = Cancel Idle Routes, Esc = Clear selection, M = Mute, K = Cast, F = Fullscreen, H = Manual, D = All Danger.
