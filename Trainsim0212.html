NX SIGNALLING CONTROL PANEL — OPERATING MANUAL

1. ENTRANCE-EXIT (NX) ROUTING PRINCIPLE

On a British Rail IECC / NX panel, you do not manually pull levers for every signal and point. Instead, you command a movement from an Entrance Signal to an Exit Point:

  • Step 1 (Entrance): Click the Red Entrance Button at the signal where the train is approaching or standing. The button will flash white/red.
  • Step 2 (Exit): Click a valid Yellow Exit Button at the destination platform, through line, or exit portal.
  • Step 3 (Interlocking): The interlocking automatically checks for conflicts, throws required points (switches), locks the route in Bright White, and clears the signal aspect (Yellow or Green).

2. CANCELLING & RELEASING ROUTES

To cancel a route that has been set (provided no train is occupying the approach locking or route track circuits):

  • Right-click or Double-click the Red Entrance button of the active route.
  • Or use CANCEL IDLE ROUTES on the bottom console (or press C).
  • Routes automatically normalize section-by-section behind a passing train (Sequential Release).

3. HEADCODES & TRAIN CLASSES

  • 1xxx — Express Passenger (Fast, Platform 1/2 or Through)
  • 2xxx — Local / Stopping Passenger (Platforms 1, 2, 3)
  • 5xxx / 6xxx — Empty Stock / Freight / Goods Loop

Train Describer (TD) berths display the 4-character headcode and step forward automatically as trains enter each track circuit.

4. INTERLOCKING SAFETY & POINT KEYS

Individual point keys (P1–P6) default to AUTO so NX routes can swing them automatically. You may key a point to N (Normal) or R (Reverse) manually—however, the interlocking will block manual movement if the point track circuit is occupied or locked by a route. In Point Failure scenarios, failed points must be bypassed using alternate platforms!

5. KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS (External Keyboard)

Space / P — Pause / Resume
1 2 4 — Simulation Speed
S — Spawn Train Now
C — Cancel Idle Routes
Esc — Clear Entrance Selection
M — Toggle Audio
H — Open / Close Manual
D — All Signals Danger
